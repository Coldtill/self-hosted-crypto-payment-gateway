# Security policy

Please report vulnerabilities privately by email to **security@coldtill.com**, with the details and steps to
reproduce. Don't open a public issue, and don't test against servers you don't own.

We reply within 3 working days and keep you updated until it's fixed. Published advisories are listed at
https://coldtill.com/security, and the same data is at https://coldtill.com/releases.json.

Security fixes are free for every edition, Free included.
